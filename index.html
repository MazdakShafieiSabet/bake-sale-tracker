Core mission: Donating effectively, taking into account where the money we donate makes the most impact. With that in mind, we want to shed light on the problem of more and more countries relapsing into illiberal or autocratic governments. Not just shed light, we also want to do something about it. Hence this fundraiser 

The NGO to which the money will be donated is called Effektiv Spenden. It is the recommended and most trusted organization for effective donating in Austria. From there, our funds get divided into the most impactful ways to defend democratic ideals. 
For example:
•Legal action (taking autocratic governments to court to defend democratic rights).
•Public mobilization (organizing campaigns to counter misinformation or political repression).
•Defending civic spaces (protecting activists, journalists, and organizations under threat)
•Analyse academic and field research to find gaps in democracy support.

What we will do: Have a bake sale every Monday Tuesday and Thursday, all thw way till Project Week. We will offer all kinds of sweet treats- cookies, banana bread, cake, ice cream and cold drinks.
